WHAT IS CREEPYPASTA?

The evolution of online horror

Creepypasta are horror stories and internet folklore that is shared online. You have probably received a message with a creepy picture stating " IF YOU DON'T SHARE THIS WITH 5 PEOPLE, THE CREATURE FIND IN YOU IN YOUR SLEEP". These were the original online/internet copypasta, copy & paste.
